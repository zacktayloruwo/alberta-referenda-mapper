Vote share: special, advance and Election Day ballots

Mail-in Ballots, called special ballots in Elections Alberta's results, are votes cast by mail. Any eligible elector could request one by September 25, 2026 without giving a reason, then return it with a signed declaration and a copy of their ID. Completed ballots had to reach Elections Alberta in Edmonton by 5 p.m. on October 16. How voting by mail works
